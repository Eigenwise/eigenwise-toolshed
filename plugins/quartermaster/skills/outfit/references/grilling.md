# Grilling

Interview the person until you both understand the same thing. Treat the problem as a **design tree**: each decision opens the decisions that hang off it.

**Facts are your job. Decisions are theirs.** Anything you can find out from the code, the docs or a tool, you find out yourself, with routed read-only agents where it helps. Never ask the person for something you could look up. Never record your own conclusion as their decision.

Work in **rounds**. The frontier is every decision whose prerequisites are settled. Ask the whole frontier in one round. A question that depends on an answer still open belongs to a later round.

**Only what is theirs.** A decision reaches the person only when it cannot be undone, spends money or reaches outside the project, or is taste or product direction only they have. Any other choice with a recommended answer is taken: say in one line what you chose and how to undo it, and move on. A question that fails this becomes a decided-by-default answer, recorded as such.

For every question:

- Lead with what you found, in a line or two, so the question stands on its own.
- Give your recommended answer and why.
- Word it so "yes" accepts the recommendation.
- Keep the project and the place with it: name the project the question belongs to, and say where it is answered (in this chat with the words to type, or in the board or inbox of that project).
- If the evidence already answers it, do not ask. Say what you decided and move on.

**Challenge before you ask.** Where the request contradicts the code, is larger than the problem needs, or has a cheaper route, say so first and suggest the alternative. Mark each suggestion Strong, Worth exploring or Speculative.

Closed choices with a clear default go through the question tool, recommended option first. Open questions go out as a numbered plain-text round the person answers by number.

If the person hands the rest to you ("use your judgement", "you decide"), stop asking. Take the recommended answer for everything left and record each as decided by default.

You are done when the frontier is empty: every branch visited, nothing silently assumed. Play the result back in a few lines and get a yes before acting on it.
