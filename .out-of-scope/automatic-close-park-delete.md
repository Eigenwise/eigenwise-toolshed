# Nothing closes, parks or deletes a ticket by its age

2026-11-19

**Asked:** tickets nobody touched for some days move to `parked` or get closed, and their worktrees and branches are removed, so the board stays short.

**Evidence:** old tickets on the board did not cause the reported failure by being there. With leftover tickets and an unrelated request, a session started one in 0 of 18 runs. What an old ticket costs is a person's attention on the pane, and that is a drawing problem. A status that changes by itself means the board says something no person and no agent said. And an unmerged branch removed on a timer is lost work, the one thing the worktree verdicts exist to prevent.

**Decision:** Age never changes a status. On the pane, old tickets group under one line with a one-press set aside or drop. Drop removes only what the worktree verdict calls safe, and keeps and names unmerged branches. Pane only, no model tokens.

**Would change if:** a real board gets so long that the grouped line stops being enough for the person to find today's work.
