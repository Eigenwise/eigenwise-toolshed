# No blocking Stop hooks

2026-11-16

**v1 had:** Stop hooks that blocked the end of a turn to push work forward, a SessionStart block that granted itself standing authorization, and a Stop-time offer answered by typing a keyword.

**Evidence:** Stop-hook blocks looped for 5 turns and for 9 turns in September. The Stop-time resupply offer was shown 63 times with no decline on record, because a line the human never acts on records nothing.

**Decision:** Nothing resumes a turn and nothing speaks for the human. Open work shows in a status entry and a pane. One offer at a real pause, for a named candidate, with a button to refuse it.

**Would change if:** a v2 failure shows finished work abandoned that a visible status entry could not have prevented.
