title: "GH-377: a direct claim can submit with a capture of the ticket's current verify"
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

A ticket taken with `claim --direct` after an earlier dispatch had no submit path. The verify-capture wrapper, capture recording, and submit all read the terminal dispatch's pinned verify, so after `update {verify}` the wrapper refused the new command and submit compared against the old one. A direct claim now uses the ticket's current verify everywhere. When no matching capture exists, the `verification_capture_required` refusal names the direct claim instead of `dispatch attempt <none>` and prints the exact `verify-capture.js --project ... --ticket ...` line to run. A capture recorded by the earlier dispatch attempt still doesn't admit the direct claim.
