---
ref: SQ-2939
title: Lifecycle calls resolve the board from the caller's own claim and worktree
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

An executor working a ticket on a sibling repository's board no longer writes its commits, submissions and comments to the spawning session's board. With `project` omitted, the board comes from the caller's own binding: the ref plus the worktree its dispatch reserved, or the ref plus the claim owner for a shared-tree dispatch. When nothing binds, the call stays on the session's board with its existing refusals unchanged.
