---
ref: SQ-2963
title: "sidequest: a stale worktree is quarantined before deletion, and a branch ref is only deleted when its tip has not moved"
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

The worktree sweep no longer deletes anything where it stands. Every reclaim renames the tree into quarantine first, and content that was there when the sweep classified it, or that arrives before the move, parks the whole tree. A rename never follows a dependency link, and the links are released at the quarantine destination after the move, so a move that cannot happen leaves the source byte-for-byte and record-for-record. The moved copy is re-read once before its files are deleted, so a file written into it in the instant after that read goes with it; a commit cannot be lost that way, because worktrees share the main object store and the sweep now deletes a branch only with `git update-ref -d` against the tip it read at the destination, keeping the branch and reporting it as `tip_moved` when anything moved the ref in between. Stale worktrees are quarantined whole for two weeks instead of being rebuilt from a patch, so unusual files, links, nested repositories, and empty directories remain intact for recovery. The sweep counts ignored content as data, with installed files under an ignored `node_modules` as the one exception, since worktree setup regenerates that cache; quarantine retention is age alone, so nothing younger than two weeks is deleted; and every delivery path, `sidequest integrate` included, reclaims the candidate worktree in the same command. Two limits are now stated on every surface rather than left to be discovered: on Windows a process still holding the tree open makes the rename fail and the tree stays put until a later pass, and quarantine lives under the Sidequest home, so a worktree on a different volume parks as `quarantine_failed` every pass until the quarantine directory is on the same volume.
