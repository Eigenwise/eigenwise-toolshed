---
ref: SQ-103
title: The pending rework section names no actor and claims a preserved ref only once it exists
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

The "Pending rework" section of a repair briefing said "<rejectedBy> sent candidate X back for rework". `rework` only accepts the candidate's owner as `by`, so that named the executor that produced the candidate, not the reviewer who rejected it. The section now says "Candidate X was sent back for rework at <time>".

When ref preservation failed, the rejection row stays `pending` but the section still said "(preserved at refs/...)". The suffix now appears only once preservation is no longer pending. The section also dropped fallbacks for a missing candidate, reason, review, or time, which no rework row can lack. The section is built from three small helpers so the CRAP gate passes.
