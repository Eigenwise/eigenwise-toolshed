---
ref: SQ-31
title: wave assembly derives its surfaces from the recorded submission snapshot instead of live scope
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Wave assembly now derives a candidate's declared surfaces from its recorded admittedScope snapshot first. It falls back to live scope only when a ticket has no snapshot yet. A post-submit scope change can no longer retroactively widen or narrow an already-submitted candidate's wave surface.
