---
ref: SQ-2922
title: Give a bound-unclaimed dispatch an exit before the idle backstop
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

A dispatch whose runtime died before its first claim now retires on `dispatch --retireOnly --recoveryEvidence` once a five-minute claim grace passes with no claim, checkpoint, or claim activity, instead of waiting out the hour-long idle backstop, and `groomClose --deliveryCommit` names that retirement as its exit. The refusal inside the grace prints the exact instant retirement is accepted, computed from the same value as the gate.
