title: Carry implementation context into executor spawns
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Dispatch prompts now carry the bounded 8 KB ticket body, 4 KB anchors, declared scope, and a 16 KB newest-first story log window before the token-gated fetch. The 16 KB window fits one maximum-size 16,000-byte handoff plus metadata; the remaining full packet stays behind the fetch. This spends at most tens of kilobytes to avoid the measured 63-425 KB rediscovery runs without reintroducing full board payloads.
