ref: SQ-3936
title: The cross-board dispatch refusal names the call that retires an unspawned prepared sibling
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

- When `dispatch` refuses because this session still holds a prepared isolated dispatch on another board (GH-665), the refusal now says that sibling keeps counting until the prepared-dispatch TTL sweep expires it, and names the exact `dispatch({ ref, project, recoveryEvidence, retireOnly: true })` call that retires it at once from the session that prepared it. Before, the only stated remedy was to wait for it to claim or end, so a dispatch you chose not to spawn blocked every other board for hours.
