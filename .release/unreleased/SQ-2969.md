The publishing repair flow now tells an orchestrator, before it dispatches a
repair, that the fresh repair ticket must link `related` to the rejected
source: plain `submit` only picks the rejected candidate as an approved
boundary when that link exists, the source's submission carries a recorded
oracle rejection, and the repair's candidate actually descends from the
rejected commit. Missing any of those leaves the repair with no legal submit
base (`duplicate_submission` then `unrecognized_base`), same as before this
was documented. Companion documentation for SQ-2757; the boundary never
extends to an active or unrelated candidate.
