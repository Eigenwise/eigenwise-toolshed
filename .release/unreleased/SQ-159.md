---
ref: SQ-159
title: groomClose advises a checkout of the observed target tip
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

`groomClose` (MCP) and `sidequest groom-close` now tell a caller to check out the observed
target tip, not a detached checkout of `deliveryCommit`: a worktree is admitted only when its
`HEAD` equals the target branch's current tip, which can be later than the delivered commit.
The `branch_not_checked_out` refusal for a recorded delivery no longer offers
`integrationBranch` / `--integration-branch`, which `groomClose` does not accept.
