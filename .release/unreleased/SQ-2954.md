---
ref: SQ-2954
title: "sidequest: quarantine stale worktrees whole, and keep quarantined work for its full two weeks"
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Stale worktrees now move whole into quarantine for two weeks instead of being rebuilt from a patch, so unusual files, links, nested repositories, and empty directories remain intact for recovery. The sweep counts ignored content as data, so a checkout whose .gitignore covered a populated nested repository is quarantined instead of removed; quarantine retention is age alone, so nothing younger than two weeks is deleted; and every delivery path, `sidequest integrate` included, reclaims the candidate worktree in the same command.
